## Design Direction

Apollo is the brand hero on a Utilitarian Bone workbench scaffold. Apollo, the clever raccoon companion to Hermes, supplies identity, voice, terracotta accent, and illustration continuity. The interface supplies warm bone surfaces, charcoal controls, editorial mastheads, hairline board columns, semantic cards, and quiet motion. Product reference remains capture → confirm → auto-save → categorized board.

## Key Decisions

- **Layout:** Instrument Serif masthead, live capture rail, and continuous five-column board for Inbox, Notizen, Links, Tasks, and Termine.
- **Palette:** Bone `#f7f6f3`; charcoal `#1a1a1a` / `#2f3437`; terracotta `#c45c3e`; white surfaces; soft category tints. Teal is informational only.
- **Typography:** Instrument Serif for display, DM Sans for UI/body, rare JetBrains Mono for timestamps and IDs.
- **Spacing and shape:** 4/8px rhythm, small radii, hairline borders, minimal shadows.
- **Interaction:** Brief capture/status feedback, restrained hover and entry motion, reduced-motion support, horizontal board scrolling when columns no longer fit.
- **Identity:** Apollo is the sole primary mark. All new character assets derive from canonical mascot reference and preserve locked DNA.
- **Assets:** 25 ready Apollo assets stay at canonical locations under `.planning/sketches/`; `pose-wave` and `pose-sleep` remain pending.
